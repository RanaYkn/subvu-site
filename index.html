Our Mission

Materials speak.
We translate.

A composite stick is characterized on the bench, then plays a season nobody measures: thousands of shots, blocks and slashes at rink temperature, in hands that load it very differently. Dynamic bending during a shot is hard to measure, laboratory instrumentation is wired and does not move from stick to stick, and every stick that fails in play is a data point no one recorded.

SubVU measures the stick itself. A printed sensor tape interfaces with the lower shaft, just above the blade-shaft junction where stress concentrates, and a small module in the butt end records the swing. Software turns the signals into per-event loading and deformation, event type, and the structural state of that stick over its life, tracked against its own first-use baseline so the method does not depend on brand, flex or kick point. It instruments any stick in minutes and transfers between sticks.

Manufacturer R&D and QA: dynamic bending on real events, layups and kick points compared on the same players, fielded sticks followed to failure.
Professional play: a structural-integrity trend for every stick and the call to replace it before it fails on the ice.
Player development: per-event loading feedback, normalized against the integrity of the stick.
