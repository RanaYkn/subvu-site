Mission

To extend the safe, useful life of carbon-fiber composite assets through real-time structural integrity monitoring, giving operators the data they need to maintain, repair, and replace components at the right time.

Vision

A world where high-value composite equipment is operated based on actual condition, not guesswork, reducing waste, downtime, and unnecessary replacement while improving safety and confidence.
