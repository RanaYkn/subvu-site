Our Mission

Materials speak.
We translate.

SubVU develops next-generation flexible sensing systems that interface directly into structural materials — capturing force, fatigue, and failure signals that have always been there but never heard.

Built at the intersection of materials science and signal intelligence, our technology bridges the gap between physical systems and the data-driven decisions that protect performance, extend asset life, and prevent costly failures.

From the ice to the grid, we make the invisible visible.
